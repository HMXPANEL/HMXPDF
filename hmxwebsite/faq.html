Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about HMXPDF.

Download status, format support, privacy, and the limits worth knowing before you rely on a document tool.

What is HMXPDF?

HMXPDF is an open-source Android document app for reading and annotating PDFs, filling forms, signing, scanning paper, editing Word and Excel documents, editing images, and creating PDFs. It is built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose and is licensed under the MIT License.

Is HMXPDF free and open source?

Yes. The source is published under the MIT License, which permits use, modification, and redistribution with the license notice. Third-party components keep their own licenses. The source is on GitHub.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 6.0 (API 23) or newer. Some features depend on the device or on Google Play services, so availability can vary even on a supported Android version.

Where can I download the APK?

Only from the official GitHub Releases page. The Download page reads that release feed and shows the current APK assets, version, date, changelog, and SHA-256 when GitHub provides them.

If the download panel says there is no public APK release yet, none has been published. HMXPDF's website does not host or mirror APK files, and it does not list debug or unsigned builds as official downloads.

Can I use HMXPDF offline?

Most PDF, spreadsheet, image, and local-document work runs on the device. HMXPDF is not fully offline in every build: the FOSS build needs a connection to download the optional Office engine or to convert a web address you enter. Google Play services may also provide scanner or background-removal components.

See the privacy policy for the complete network list.

Can I edit Word, PowerPoint, or existing PDF text?

DOCX files have an in-app editor for paragraphs, tables, formatting, comments, tracked changes, headers and footers, page setup, and find-and-replace. It is not full Microsoft Word parity, and complex parts may open read-only.

PPT and PPTX files are viewed and can be exported as PDF; they are not edited in their original format. PDFs can be annotated, signed, have forms filled, and have pages organized, but text already printed into a PDF page is not rewritten.

Which spreadsheet formats can be edited?

XLSX is the spreadsheet format HMXPDF can edit and save. XLS and ODS files are opened through the viewer, and visual fidelity varies by file. CSV and TXT files can also be opened, but they do not have XLSX cell editing and save support.

Does HMXPDF collect my documents or usage data?

HMXPDF has no account system, no in-app advertising, no analytics SDK, and no crash-reporting service in the app code. It does not operate a server for uploading documents or extracted text. Recents, local document copies, checkpoints, signatures, and settings are stored on your device.

A few optional features contact the network, including the Office engine download, web-address conversion, and Google Play services components. The privacy policy explains each one.

Are the signatures cryptographic digital signatures?

No. HMXPDF lets you save a signature image and place it on a page. It does not create a certificate-based digital signature, verify a signer's identity, or provide a timestamp authority. Use a dedicated e-signature service if you need those guarantees.

Do I need Google Play services?

Not for the core PDF, page, image, and local-document tools. The document scanner is provided by Google Play services, and background removal uses an ML Kit model that may be delivered by Play services. Some OCR components and read-aloud also depend on the device. Without those components, the related feature may be unavailable, but other tools continue to work.

Where are exported files saved?

New exports go to Download/HMXPDF by default, or to a folder you choose through Android's file picker. Files you save there remain until you delete them. Uninstalling the app does not delete exports stored outside its private storage.

Why does an Office document look different in the app?

Office rendering is complex and depends on the format and renderer. The optional LibreOffice-based engine can improve fidelity for supported Office formats. In the FOSS build it is downloaded only when you request it in Settings, after its size and SHA-256 are checked; it is not bundled in the GitHub APKs.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Open an issue on the official issue tracker. Include the app version from Settings, your Android version, the device model, the distribution (GitHub APK or Play build), and clear reproduction steps. Remove private document content from screenshots and logs.

For a security concern, avoid posting exploit details publicly; ask for a private channel in a minimal issue first. See Support.

Is there an iOS, web, or desktop version?

No. HMXPDF is an Android application. This website provides information, release status, privacy details, and support links; it is not a browser-based document editor.

Can I use HMXPDF commercially?

The MIT License permits commercial use, modification, and redistribution, provided the copyright and permission notice are retained. The license does not grant rights to the HMXPDF name, the app icon, or third-party trademarks. Third-party components remain under their own licenses.
