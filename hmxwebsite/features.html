Feature guide

Verified PDF and document tools, with honest limits.

This page describes what the HMXPDF Android app actually implements, based on the public source. Features that depend on the device, Google Play services, or an optional engine are marked as such.

PDF

Read and search PDFs

Open a PDF and read it with layouts that make sense on a phone, from continuous scrolling to single-page book mode.

  • • Continuous scrolling and pinch-to-zoom
  • • Page navigation and bookmarks
  • • In-document text search
  • • Text selection and copy
  • • Optional dark pages
  • • Auto-scroll for hands-free reading

Also available

Image-only pages can be processed with OCR to extract text on the device.

Markup

Annotate, fill forms, and sign

Review a document and mark it up without printing it. Annotations are exported into a copy of the PDF, and saved signatures are stored in app-private storage.

Text markup

Highlight, underline, and strike through text.

Drawing

Ink, shapes, arrows, and free text notes.

Signatures

Save a signature image and place it on a page.

PDF forms

Fill form fields or flatten them into the page.

HMXPDF does not rewrite arbitrary text already printed into a PDF page. It annotates, fills forms, and edits pages around the existing content.

Paper to digital

Scan documents and recognize text

Scan paper with the Google Play services document scanner, review the captured pages, and save them as a PDF. For image-only PDFs, the OCR engine extracts text on the device.

Multi-page capture

Capture pages and adjust the results before saving.

On-device OCR

Bundled ML Kit text recognition with a Tesseract fallback.

Image cleanup

Crop, straighten, and correct scanned images.

The scanner is provided by Google Play services and may not be available on every device. OCR accuracy depends on the source image and language.

Word

Open and edit DOCX documents

A .docx opens as a document with true page proportions, margins, and the page breaks Word itself recorded. Switch to edit mode to change paragraphs and table cells in place, then save the changes back to the .docx.

  • • Paragraph and table-cell editing
  • • Formatting bar and styles
  • • Comments and tracked changes
  • • Headers, footers, notes
  • • Page setup and paper size
  • • Find and replace
  • • Spell check and read aloud
  • • Save, export PDF, or print

This is a focused document editor, not full Microsoft Word parity. Complex layouts, fields, or tracked content may open in a read-only form for some parts. Word is a trademark of Microsoft Corporation; HMXPDF is not affiliated with Microsoft.

Spreadsheets

Edit and save XLSX workbooks

XLSX is the spreadsheet format HMXPDF can edit and save. Tap a cell to change its value or formula, format it, and write the workbook back to its original file.

  • • Cell values and formulas
  • • Bold, italic, underline, strike
  • • Text and fill colours
  • • Number, currency, percent, date formats
  • • Alignment, wrapping, borders
  • • Validation dropdowns
  • • Insert or delete rows and columns
  • • Undo, redo, and export PDF

Only XLSX workbooks have cell editing and save support. XLS, ODS, and CSV files are opened through the viewer; visual fidelity varies by file.

Formats

Open common Office and text files

HMXPDF can open PDF, DOC/DOCX, XLS/XLSX, PPT/PPTX, ODT/ODS/ODP, RTF, CSV, and TXT files. Rendering depends on the format and the renderer in use.

Word

DOC and DOCX can be viewed; DOCX has the in-app editor.

Spreadsheets

XLSX can be edited and saved. XLS and ODS are viewed.

Presentations

PPT and PPTX are viewed and can be exported as PDF.

Optional LibreOffice-based engine

An optional engine can improve rendering for supported Office formats. In the FOSS build it is downloaded only when you request it in Settings, after its size and SHA-256 are checked. The Play build uses on-demand feature delivery. The engine is not bundled in the GitHub APKs.

Images

Edit images without another app

Adjust a photo or a scanned page before sending it into a PDF. The image editor covers common cleanup and markup tasks.

  • • Crop, rotate, straighten
  • • Perspective correction
  • • Colour and tone adjustments
  • • Filters
  • • Draw and add text
  • • Add a watermark
  • • Resize an image
  • • Remove a background*
  • • Open PNG, JPEG, WebP, BMP, HEIC

* Background removal uses ML Kit subject segmentation on supported devices; the model may be delivered by Google Play services.

Create

Make a PDF from local content

Build a PDF from images, typed text, or a local HTML file. The FOSS build can also load a web address you enter and render that page as a PDF; the request goes to the site you chose, not to an HMXPDF document-processing server.

From images

Combine photos or scans into one document.

From text or HTML

Local content is rendered without loading a website.

From a web address

FOSS build only; the page and its resources are requested.

Read this before you rely on a feature

Honest limits

  • Printed PDF text is not rewritten. You can annotate, sign, fill form fields, and manage pages, but HMXPDF does not reflow text that is already printed into a PDF.
  • DOCX editing is focused, not Word parity. Complex documents may have parts that open read-only. Review important files after saving.
  • Only XLSX supports spreadsheet editing and saving. Other spreadsheet formats are viewed, and fidelity varies.
  • Presentations are view and export only. PPT and PPTX are not edited in their original format.
  • Some features need Google Play services. Scanning, subject segmentation, and some ML Kit components may be unavailable on devices without them. Read-aloud depends on the device text-to-speech engine.
  • Office fidelity varies. The optional LibreOffice-based engine can improve rendering, but it is a separate download and not bundled in the GitHub APKs.
