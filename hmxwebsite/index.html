One app, many document jobs

A document toolkit, not just a viewer.

Open a PDF, sign a form, scan a page, or make a quick edit to a Word or Excel file. HMXPDF keeps the common document tasks close together.

Read and search PDFs

Continuous scrolling, pinch-to-zoom, page navigation, bookmarks, dark pages, and in-document search. Select and copy text as you read.

Annotate and sign

Highlight, underline, strike through, draw, add shapes or text notes, and place a saved signature. Export an annotated copy when you are done.

Organize and convert

Merge, split, extract, reorder, rotate, compress, encrypt, decrypt, watermark, and add page numbers. Convert PDFs to images or text, and images to PDF.

Scan and OCR

Capture multi-page documents with the Google Play services scanner, or run OCR on image-only PDF pages with the bundled on-device engine.

Edit Word documents

Open DOCX files with true page proportions, edit paragraphs and table cells in place, apply formatting, work with comments and tracked changes, then save or export to PDF.

Work with spreadsheets

Edit XLSX cells and formulas, apply number formats and styles, use validation dropdowns, insert rows or columns, and save the workbook back to XLSX.

PDF printed text is not rewritten in place, and Office fidelity varies by document. See the full feature list and limits.

Private by implementation

Your documents stay on your device.

HMXPDF has no account system, no in-app advertising, no analytics SDK, and no crash-reporting service in the app code. It does not operate a server for uploading your files or extracted text.

A few optional features use the network: the FOSS build can download a pinned Office engine or render a web address you type, and Google Play services may provide the scanner and background-removal models.

Read the privacy policy

  • No account or sign-in

    Open the app and work with a file. Nothing to register.

  • No ads, analytics, or crash SDK

    These components are not included in the app code.

  • No document upload server

    Document processing runs in the app, not on an HMXPDF service.
